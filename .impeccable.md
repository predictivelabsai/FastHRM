# Design Context — FastHRM (part of the FastSME suite)

## Target audience
Estonian small & medium businesses, startups, and their HR/office managers and
founders. Secondary: accountants and operations leads who run payroll and people
admin. Users are pragmatic, price-sensitive, and expect Estonian-first, locally
compliant software (TÖR, TSD, Smart-ID/Mobile-ID). Many are switching from
spreadsheets or expensive incumbents (Persona, Wemply, HRM4Baltics).

## Use cases
Run employee records, leave & attendance, Estonian statutory payroll, recruiting/
ATS, performance and lifecycle — in one place, self-hosted or hosted for €1/seat.
Open-source (MIT), part of the FastSME suite (47 products).

## Brand personality / tone
Confident, modern, Nordic-clean, friendly-but-serious, transparent (open-source,
honest comparisons). Not corporate-stiff, not playful-toy. "Serious software that
doesn't cost a fortune."

## Visual direction (locked 2026-09-09)
- **Anchor:** confident **dark hero** (forest-ink, tinted — never pure black) with
  a bright **lime/chartreuse** accent, resolving into warm off-white ("paper")
  light sections. One bold dark statement, then airy and clean — NOT glow-on-dark
  everywhere, no glassmorphism.
- **Accent model:** green/lime is FastHRM's accent, but the design system exposes a
  per-product `--accent` token so every FastSME product can set its own.
- **Hero visual:** stylized product-UI mockups built in HTML/CSS that **mirror the
  real FastHRM app** (locked 2026-09-09 after user feedback): light theme, white top
  bar with the "Fast HRM" logo, light grouped sidebar (OVERVIEW / PEOPLE / TIME / PAY /
  TALENT / PERFORMANCE / LIFECYCLE with tiny uppercase labels and small colourful
  icons), KPI cards with uppercase labels and coloured right-edge accents, the
  horizontal "Headcount by department" bar chart, leave-request tables with type
  pills, and the AI Assistant panel. The mockup must be recognisable as this
  product, not an invented dark-sidebar dashboard.
- **Language:** bilingual **Estonian + English** with a working switcher; Estonian
  is the default/primary.
- **Type:** Bricolage Grotesque (display) + Hanken Grotesk (body). Avoid Inter/
  Roboto/system defaults. Must cover Estonian glyphs (õ ä ö ü š ž).
- **Buttons:** ink-filled on light sections (accessible), lime reserved for the dark
  hero and highlights (lime text fails contrast on white — never body/CTA text).
- **Copy voice (locked 2026-09-09):** humanized, plain language. No em dashes, no
  AI clichés (no "vibrant", "seamless", "robust", "empower", "elevate", no forced
  rule-of-three, no "not just X, it's Y"). Estonian reads like a person wrote it;
  short sentences, concrete claims, no marketing padding.
- **Comparison tables:** FastHRM is always the first (leftmost) product column.
  Pricing is shown for FastHRM only; competitors' pricing cells stay empty because
  their pricing is inquiry-based or gated.

## Anti-patterns to avoid
Cyan-on-dark and purple→blue gradients (the old FastHRM cyan is being replaced),
neon-glow dark mode, identical repeating icon+heading+text card grids, gradient
text on headings/metrics, everything-in-a-card, centered-everything, oversized
dead whitespace between sections (the logo strip + section-heading stack was
redesigned 2026-09-09 for this reason).
